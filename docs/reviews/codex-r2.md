8. **Blocker — permissionless round creation can permanently squat every listed ticker.** [`src/SummonFactory.sol:84`](src/SummonFactory.sol:84) accepts any future `deadline`; [`src/SummonRound.sol:163`](src/SummonRound.sol:163) only requires it exceed now; and [`src/SummonFactory.sol:91`](src/SummonFactory.sol:91) prevents another round while it remains Open. An attacker can call `openRound(token, hugeTarget, type(uint64).max, ...)` for every listed token at no cost. No pledges are needed, and each ticker is blocked for effectively forever. Bound deadlines relative to creation, constrain target/lock configuration, and add a permissionless cancellation/replacement path for unpledged stalled rounds.

9. **Major — the new KV-total design is an unbounded, eventually consistent public read path.** [`web/worker/index.ts:70`](web/worker/index.ts:70) lists every historical `i:` key and performs one serial KV get per key for every board GET; expired records are never removed. A modest Sybil can create enough records that every board load, demand export, and some POST responses incur hundreds/thousands of KV operations, exceed Worker limits, or become prohibitively slow/costly. KV list/value propagation can also temporarily show a replaced `$250` intent after its `$1` replacement. Move serialized intent state and expiring aggregates to a Durable Object/D1 table, expire records with an alarm/job, and paginate issuer exports.

10. **Major — the documented direct-Pyth deployment cannot be operated through the round page.** [`web/src/round.ts:435`](web/src/round.ts:435) and [`:443`](web/src/round.ts:443) always submit empty update arrays and zero ETH. For `PRICE_SOURCE=pyth`, a page-started auction or fill therefore reads the stale onchain Pyth value and reverts, despite the backend script correctly fetching update data at [`ts/e2e.ts:43`](ts/e2e.ts:43). Provide a server-side update-data endpoint/relayer plus fee quote, or explicitly disable these actions for direct-Pyth rounds and direct fillers to a supported flow.

11. **Minor — permanent high-water nonces can permanently lock out a legitimate wallet.** [`web/src/intents.ts:24`](web/src/intents.ts:24) uses client-clock milliseconds, while [`web/worker/index.ts:159`](web/worker/index.ts:159) stores the maximum forever. A user with a clock accidentally years ahead, or who signs a high nonce through another interface, receives 409 for every ordinary future intent until their clock catches up. Issue the next nonce from the serialized intent store, rather than trusting an unbounded client-chosen number.
